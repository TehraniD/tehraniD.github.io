Final Jeopardy: The Body's Greatest Betrayal
This autoimmune condition is nicknamed "the great imitator" because it mimics so many other diseases. It involves Type III hypersensitivity with immune complex deposition, and patients classically present with a facial rash that looks like a butterfly landed on their nose. It can attack the joints, kidneys, skin, and brain.
