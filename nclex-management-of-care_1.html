How to work this

  1. Read What it is really testing first. Do not skip to the question. The test plan statement is a verb plus an object — the verb tells you which clinical judgment step is being measured, and that is the whole game.
  2. Read What the nurse actually does and say it out loud in your own words. If you cannot say it without reading it, you do not have it yet.
  3. Answer the practice item before revealing the rationale. Commit to an option. Guessing and then reading is how people memorize rationales instead of building judgment.
  4. After the rationale, answer this out loud: what would have to change in the stem for a different option to become correct? That question is where the transfer happens.
